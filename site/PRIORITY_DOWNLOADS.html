Downloads to distinguish tied top-50 researchers

93 missing articles could distinguish at least one currently tied researcher pair near the top 50. The focus includes 66 authors by total count and 52 by US count, retaining all ties at rank 50.

For each paper we simulate one additional US-article credit for every first/last author simultaneously. We count equal-US pairs that separate within the US top 50, and equal-total/equal-US pairs that become distinguishable on US evidence within the total-count top 50. A shared article cannot separate two authors who both receive its credit. Total article counts do not change. This is a possible outcome, not a prediction; a non-US resolution adds no US credit, and a US resolution may create new ties elsewhere.

Each ten-paper batch greedily covers previously uncovered tied pairs. Ties in this priority use a weight favoring pairs closer to the top, then the number of pairs and Scopus ID. Papers already saved locally and papers reported unavailable are omitted. The CSV retains both separated and newly created US pairs. Recompute priorities after each review round.
