Does extending to 2010 reduce ties?

The search covers 2010–2026, adding 2010–2015 to the frozen 2016–2026 snapshot. The journal frame and positive query phrases are unchanged. The extension adds 493 distinct articles in the frame; eight researchers enter and eight leave the displayed 100-person pool.

Does the longer window reduce ties? Overall article-count ties barely change in the displayed dashboards. US-count ties fall modestly, and the highest US count rises. The cutoff remains crowded.
