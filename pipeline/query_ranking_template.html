How to interpret the US columns

US counts combine explicit and contextual sample evidence. Unclear and unreviewed articles remain separate; neither is treated as evidence of a non-US sample. The upper count adds both groups to the identified US count. Geography is about study participants, not the author's institution. An article with US and non-US samples contributes once.

The historical US ranking covered only the existing 100-person list. The new US tables expose all retrieved first/last authors, with annotation coverage shown. Sorting partially reviewed counts can favor better-reviewed authors; the report describes the coverage achieved and remaining limitations.
