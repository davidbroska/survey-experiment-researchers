# Independent RA verification draft

The local verification packet is generated by `python3 score/verify.py` after all obtainable articles have received a primary full-text review. No invitations or assignments have been sent.

The random packet contains 62 articles from the 51 journals with verified full text: one random article per journal, plus one additional article in 11 randomly selected journals with at least two available articles. Selection within each journal is uniform without replacement using seed 20261007. Eleven journals currently lack obtainable full text, so this is not one article from each of the 62 journals. The private manifest lists them. The draw includes eligible and ineligible AI reference classes without selecting on those labels. A separate targeted packet contains up to 20 additional disagreements or unresolved cases; it is for checking difficult rules and must not be pooled with the random packet for performance estimates.

Give reviewers `private/score/ra_random_blind.csv` and the linked source documents. Give the targeted packet separately. Keep `ra_selection_key.csv`, AI predictions, AI reference labels, and prior rationales away from reviewers until their independent decisions are saved. Have two RAs independently code each article, then resolve disagreements with a third reviewer. Record both initial decisions and the adjudication rather than overwriting initial labels.

For every article, inspect the methods of all studies, including pilots and pretests, and relevant supporting materials or repositories. Record the source location and a short exact excerpt supporting collection and the team's collection or commissioning role. Earlier data count only when the team's role is evidenced; author overlap or institutional affiliation alone does not establish that role. Keep unresolved provenance UNCLEAR rather than forcing a binary answer.

Use YES, NO, or UNCLEAR separately for collection, eligible experiment, and eligible survey. A qualifying survey is a study primarily designed as a questionnaire survey, including survey experiments and repeated diary surveys. Incidental questionnaires and clinical scales within laboratory tasks or interventions do not qualify as primary surveys. A separate primary-survey pilot can qualify. A natural experiment does not establish participant collection. There is no restriction on participants' country, researchers' location, statistical significance, provider, or platform. These are article-level eligibility labels, not a count of individual participants or evidence that each coauthor personally collected data.

Random verification does not remove access bias or the pilot's retention of existing articles. Report coverage, unresolved cases, and class-specific agreement with actual denominators. Use the private selection probabilities if weighting the available-article sample; those weights do not recover inaccessible articles. Report targeted-case findings separately.
