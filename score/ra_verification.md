# Independent RA verification

The primary reference assessment is a thorough AI source review. RAs will verify a sample after the source assessments have been updated for the primary digital-questionnaire scope. The existing 288 reviews use broader criteria. No new questionnaire-focused review packet has been drawn or assigned; the earlier blank drafts and their obsolete generator were removed.

For the current 620-article collection, draw one article uniformly from each of the 62 journals after source review is complete. Each journal has ten articles, so each has a 1/10 selection probability within this collection. Save the seed, selected IDs and actual sampling probabilities with the packet. Do not select the random packet on eligibility labels.

Draw a separate, disjoint targeted packet of up to 20 unresolved or disputed cases. Use it to check difficult definitions; do not pool it with the random packet when estimating agreement or error rates.

Provide the selected articles and source documents with blank fields for team collection, primary-survey design, participant digital completion and in-scope survey experiments. Use the [new prompt's definitions](prompt_proposed.md), while allowing full-text reviewers to inspect methods, supplements and repositories beyond the metadata supplied to the screening model. Record exact evidence locations, brief excerpts, reasoning, reviewer and date. Keep AI decisions and prior rationales hidden until the independent responses are saved.

Inspect all reported studies, including distinct survey pilots and pretests. Establish collection or commissioning responsibility and the actual questionnaire interface. The same study must satisfy the collection, primary-survey and digital criteria. Earlier-team reuse needs evidence of the original collection role and procedure. Missing evidence remains UNCLEAR. A NO cannot rest on inspecting only an ineligible component of a mixed article.

Have two RAs independently review the sampled papers and resolve disagreements with recorded adjudication. Preserve both initial decisions and the resolution. Report the random and targeted samples separately, including unresolved cases and actual denominators. Random verification within this collection does not remove the accessibility and selection limitations of the pilot.
